Established in Taiwan in 1984, Flytech has built a solid foundation in hardware design and manufacturing, 
specializing in Point-of-Sale Terminals, Self-Service Kiosks, Touch Panel PCs, and Embedded Computing Systems for 
the retail, hospitality, medical, and industrial sectors worldwide.
As a pioneer in embracing AI technology, we have evolved into a domain-focused solutions provider by 
integrating AI with our hardware platforms to deliver intelligent, high-value solutions. This forward-looking 
transformation empowers global customers to optimize operations, enhance user experiences, and stay ahead in a 
rapidly changing market.
Our passion lies in developing tailored innovations that solve real-world challenges. With a commitment to 
solution-driven technology and customer-centric service, we enable businesses to grow and lead through 
continuous innovation.
